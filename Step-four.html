Reading in 2025

A short summary of my year so far

Robert Galbraith - Lethal White

Lethal White is a Cormoran Strike novel where private investigator Cormoran Strike and his partner Robin Ellacott investigate a man's claim of witnessing a child's strangling years earlier, which leads them into a complex murder plot involving wealthy families, political corruption in Parliament, and a sinister manor house. The investigation is complicated by Strike's newfound public fame and Robin's personal struggles, including trauma from past cases and her fraught relationship with her husband.

Robert Galbraith - Ink black heart

The Ink Black Heart is a novel in Robert Galbraith's (J.K. Rowling's) Cormoran Strike series about private detective Robin Ellacott becoming involved in a murder case linked to the victim's popular animated cartoon and a toxic online fandom. After Edie Ledwell, the cartoon's co-creator, approaches Robin about being harassed by a mysterious online figure named Anomie, Robin initially declines to help but later investigates the murder of Edie, delving into a complex world of online aliases, online games, and fan behavior.

James Swallow - Exile

In James Swallow's Exile, disgraced former MI6 agent Marc Dane is pulled back into action to stop a threat involving a Serbian gang selling fake nuclear weapons, a disgraced Russian general with access to the real deal, and a vengeful Somali warlord who wants to burn the world down. Racing from a CIA prison to Dubai and the African coast, Dane must uncover a conspiracy and prevent a nuclear catastrophe, all while battling a jaded government agency that struggles to act.
